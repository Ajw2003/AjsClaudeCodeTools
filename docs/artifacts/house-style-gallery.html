Ledger

Lead with the fact, not the framing. Sentence fragments are fine where they are clearer than a full sentence. Cut every phrase that only signals effort - no 'it is worth noting', no 'importantly', no restating the question before answering it. If the content has more than two parallel items, it is a table. Numbers get units and a basis. Where something is unknown, write unknown rather than a paragraph explaining that it is hard to know.

Prose

Quarry

Warm paper and an editorial serif, set narrow for reading at length.

Write in measured, complete sentences and let the reasoning show. Say why before what where the why is not obvious. Prefer a paragraph to a bullet list when the ideas connect, and reach for a table only when the data really is tabular. Contractions are fine; hedging is not - if something is uncertain, name the uncertainty rather than softening every sentence around it. Assume the reader has time and wants to understand, not to skim.

Signal Space Grotesk / Inter / Space Mono

Landing pages

Signal

Dark-first, electric violet, generous radius and a dramatic scale.

One idea per line. Start sentences with the verb where you can. Keep them short enough to read at a glance and vary the length so it does not read like a list pretending to be prose. Claims land harder without qualifiers, so make fewer claims and stand behind each one. A number beats an adjective. Cut the closing summary - if the page needs one, the page is too long.
